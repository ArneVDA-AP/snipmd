## Verdict

R2-M1 closed: yes. 36 URL disguises went through `isWebUrl` and `cachedUrl`: explicit `:80`/`:0080`/empty port, backslash, tab or newline in the host, uppercase, one or two trailing dots, `%2E` and `%61` in the host, userinfo (`x@`, `x:y@`, `evil.com@`), fullwidth letters, ideographic full stop, soft hyphen, zero-width space, `http:/host`, `http:host`, `http:\\host`, leading space or C0 control, `h\ttp:`, `//asset.localhost`, `asset:`, `https://asset.localhost`, and the tauri/ipc hosts. Each one either parsed to an app host and was refused, or failed the parse. None was returned unchanged. Those 36 disguises, each in 29 carriers, also went through `renderRead`, run twice so the cache was warm. The carriers were raw `<img>`, entity-encoded `src`/`href`, `<picture><source>`, `<svg><image href|xlink:href>`, `<a>`, `<area>`, `<image>`, `<input type=image>`, `<video poster/src>`, `<table background>`, `<math href>`, `srcset`, `<base>`, `<object>`, `<embed>`, `<link>`, `<meta refresh>`, `<form action>`, `formaction`, Markdown inline image and link, angle-bracket destinations, reference-style image and link, autolinks, GFM bare URLs, and a forged `data-own`. The result was 0 elements with an app-host or non-http(s) `src`/`href` and 0 surviving `srcset`/`poster`/`background`/`action`/`data` attributes. The only asset URL in any output was the one Rust resolved. On the Rust side, `Url::parse` plus `host()` maps every disguise to `asset.localhost`, `tauri.localhost` or `ipc.localhost`, and `open_link` refuses all of them. The navigation guard accepts `tauri.localhost` (any spelling), but no note link can reach it: the sanitizer strips such an `href` (`isWebUrl` is false for app hosts), and the click handler sends every non-`#` link to `open_link`. That also closes R2-m2. What remains is defense in depth (minors below).
